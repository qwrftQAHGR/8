## Virility
- **Immense stamina:** many rounds in a row, almost no recovery time, copious ejaculation, easy arousal.

## Fertility
- **Near-certain conception:** whenever conception is anatomically possible in the act, whether {{user}} is siring or receiving, it almost always happens.
- **Contraception is useless.** Condoms tear, pills fail, herbal teas and anti-pregnancy ofuda do nothing. A god's musubi does not care.
- **Both at once:** {{user}} can sire children while carrying one. A pregnant partner cannot conceive again until after giving birth, and the same goes for {{user}}.
- **Wombs:** a partner without a womb (most men and femboys, for example) cannot carry. They can still sire on {{user}}, and non-reproductive sex is simply sex.
- **Normally infertile beings can conceive:** tsukumogami, certain yokai, and old beings long past childbearing can all have children with {{user}}.

## The One-Week Pregnancy
- **Seven days** from conception to birth, whether the partner or {{user}} is carrying.
- **How it progresses:**

  | Day | Stage |
  |---|---|
  | 1 | Nothing visible; warmth, cravings |
  | 2 | A slight swell |
  | 3 | An obvious bump |
  | 4 | Looks five to six months along |
  | 5 | Big and kicking |
  | 6 | Full term and heavy |
  | 7 | Birth |

- **A healthy pregnancy:** essentially no morning sickness, energy stays high, appetite is enormous, and libido is often higher than usual. The comedy is in the logistics: clothes, coworkers, a samurai trying to fit into armor.
- **Birth:** quick (about an hour), uncomplicated and safe, more intense than painful. It goes well wherever it happens. Yaegiri the Yamauba (see 06) senses every birth tied to {{user}} and can step through any door to act as midwife. Giving birth in the Mayoiga is easiest.
- **Number of children:** usually one. Twins are occasional. Species prone to litters (some beastfolk, inugami, nekomata) may have up to three.
- **Recovery:** bodies recover within a day with no lasting toll, except {{user}}'s breasts (below).

## Breasts and Lactation
- **Growth:** every birth {{user}} carries permanently increases {{user}}'s breast size by about one cup size, overnight after the birth. There is no upper limit.
- **Siring does not count.** Only births {{user}} carries make the breasts grow.
- **The body adapts:** no back pain, and the breasts stay shapely and healthy, because they are god-made. Clothing and bras turn into a running comic problem.
- **Lactation:** {{user}} starts lactating after their first birth, and the supply increases with each one. The milk is sweet and nourishing and carries a tiny temporary blessing: a day of pep and good luck for whoever drinks it.
- Partners who give birth lactate normally.

## The Libido (Musubi Pressure)
Musubi builds up in {{user}} all the time. It feels like arousal, heat and restlessness, like water rising behind a dam.

- **Real release:** sex with a living partner releases it. Each climax shared with a partner drains a lot of pressure, and a good session brings {{user}} back to baseline.
- **Masturbation** releases only a little, and the released musubi goes somewhere nearby. The closest plant bursts into bloom, a cup of cold tea starts steaming, the mice in the walls suddenly have babies, the old umbrella in the closet twitches. This is played for comedy.
- **Pressure stages, by time since the last real release:**
  - *Just released:* a pleasant, warm, everyday horniness. {{user}} functions normally.
  - *About 12 to 24 hours:* constant arousal and distraction, frequent erections, a flushed face, trouble focusing.
  - *About 2 days:* musubi fever. Sweating and single-mindedness. Leaks begin (below).
  - *About 4 to 5 days:* delirium and near-overwhelming urges. Leaks become dramatic, and the aragami drift begins.
  - *More than a week:* real danger of turning into a wild god.
- **Need, not compulsion.** The libido makes {{user}} desperate and very wanting. It never takes over {{user}}'s will or forces {{user}} on anyone. Partners are usually happy to help, and Inaba will nag.
- Pregnancy does not reduce the libido.

## Leaks: What Unspent Musubi Does
Once the pressure is high, musubi leaks into the world around {{user}}, and the effects escalate with the pressure. They are noticeable and comic:

- **Plants:** houseplants bolt, flowers bloom out of season, sprouts come up in {{user}}'s footprints, and grass pushes through the tatami.
- **Animals:** stray cats follow {{user}} home, birds nest on {{user}}'s balcony, pets go into heat, and the mice multiply.
- **Objects:** old belongings begin twitching toward tsukumogami-hood. A 99-year-old kettle wakes up a year early and has opinions.
- **Weather:** the air smells like spring, warm breezes blow in December, and cherry blossoms open in October around {{user}}'s home.
- **Yokai and kami:** minor yokai follow the scent, and small local kami show up out of curiosity.
- **People:** people near {{user}} feel unaccountably warm and cheerful, and couples nearby get frisky. This is mood only; nobody's mind is controlled.

## The Aragami Drift
If the pressure goes unrelieved for a long time, the musubi starts filling the space where {{user}}'s self should be. {{user}} slowly begins turning into a wild fertility god (*aragami*, 荒神).

- **Signs (eerie, but comic):**
  - {{user}}'s voice picks up an echo;
  - {{user}} slips into archaic speech without meaning to;
  - animals bow as {{user}} passes;
  - old ladies leave rice balls and cups of sake at {{user}}'s feet and clap twice;
  - a sacred straw rope (shimenawa) is found tied around {{user}}'s waist in the morning and nobody knows who tied it;
  - {{user}} has gaps in their memory and a sense that the land is listening to them;
  - flowers open wherever {{user}} sits.
- **The end point** would be {{user}} becoming a mountain's god, a mindless force of spring.
- **Reversal:** spending the musubi through sex with a partner reverses every sign immediately, and one long session fully restores {{user}}.
- In this lighthearted setting the drift stays a looming threat and a comic reason to get out of the house. It is rarely a destination.
