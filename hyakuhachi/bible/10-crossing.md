# 10 · Crossing Between Worlds

## Thin Places and Ōmagatoki
- **Thin places** are spots where Omote and Ura lie close together:
  - old torii at forest edges;
  - abandoned shrines and mountain passes;
  - certain bridges, tunnels and crossroads;
  - the backs of very old bathhouses;
  - the path behind Musubi Jinja (see 07).
- **When they open:** most of the time a thin place does nothing. At **ōmagatoki** (逢魔時, "the hour of meeting demons"), the dusk between day and night, someone who walks through at the right moment in the right frame of mind (lost, sad, wishing, distracted) may cross. Crossings also happen in thick fog, during festivals, and on nights when the Night Parade passes.
- **Kamikakushi (spiriting-away)** is what Omote calls an accidental crossing. People vanish, and sometimes they come back.
- **It runs both ways.** Omote people fall into Ura, and Ura people fall into Omote.
- **Natural crossing is unreliable.** You can't choose where you end up. You usually arrive at the matching thin place on the other side, or somewhere nearby.

## Rules for Travelers
- **Same time in both worlds.** Date, time of day and season are the same on both sides. No time is lost or gained by crossing.
- **Language:** Ura speaks Japanese. It is old-fashioned, period-drama Japanese, but fully understandable to an Omote native, and the reverse is also true. Omote slang amuses people in Ura, and Ura's formal speech amuses Omote people. The script is older and harder to read. Foreigners at Ura-Dejima speak their own languages, and many also speak Ura Japanese.
- **Omote technology in Ura** works if it is self-contained. Phones work offline (photos, saved music) until the battery dies. Ura has no electricity. Comic exception: a sufficiently annoyed raijū can charge a phone, and might also fry it. There is no signal or internet. Lighters, flashlights and medicine all work, and are valuable.
- **Ura magic in Omote** is muffled (see 07). Yokai powers are dimmer and illusions thinner. Gods' power is the exception, which includes {{user}}'s musubi, the blessing, the Mayoiga and Inaba's abilities.
- **Being noticed:**
  - *Ura beings in Omote* stand out. Most disguise themselves with shapeshifting or human clothes and hats, or pass as cosplayers (Akihabara and Harajuku are very convenient). The Kakure Neighborhood Association is not thrilled.
  - *Omote-bito in Ura* stand out through their clothes, mannerisms and gadgets, and draw curiosity wherever they go.
- **Carrying things across:** objects can be carried both ways. Omote goods are valuable in Ura. Ura goods are curios in Omote, such as an Edo coin that is suspiciously new or a living lantern.
- **Money:** yen is worthless in Ura except as a curiosity, though a ¥500 coin makes a lovely charm. Ura coins are only collector's items in Omote. Drift goods are the best trade currency.
