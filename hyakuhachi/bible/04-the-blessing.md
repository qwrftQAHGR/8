# 04 · The Blessing

## The Blessing: How It Works
In Shinto, *musubi* (産霊 / 結び) means both a knot and the generative power that makes things grow and come into being. When musubi passes from {{user}} into a partner during sex, it **ripens what the partner already is**. It brings out what is already there and never turns anyone into something they are not.

- **Thread-partners: the full blessing.**
  - It begins the first time they have sex with {{user}} and is permanent.
  - It deepens with repeated intimacy, carrying them toward the next stage of their own nature.
  - After that it continues as lasting vitality and good fortune.
- **Non-thread lovers: a favor.**
  - A smaller, temporary version: a few days to a week of the general effects, plus a faint hint of the deeper ripening.
  - Regular non-thread lovers keep the favor for as long as they keep coming back, but it never becomes the full blessing.
- **Timing:** the blessing shows within hours, usually by the next morning.
- **No marks of any kind.** The blessing shows only through the body, abilities, behavior, luck, and how the world reacts to the person.
- **General effects on everyone:** vitality, robust health, glossy hair and a fresh complexion, deep sleep, better luck, and minor ailments cured (a bad knee, a chronic cough, a stammer from nerves).
- **Children of {{user}}** are born already blessed (see 05).

## Blessing: Yokai and Beastfolk
The blessing gives each kind of being more of what it is. Some examples:

- **Kitsune:** advances them toward their next tail, which normally takes a century. A few nights with {{user}} is worth decades, and fox-fire and illusions grow stronger.
- **Tanuki:** transformations hold for days instead of hours and don't slip when they're startled, and their leaf-money stays money longer.
- **Nekomata / bakeneko:** a split second tail, or a stronger hold over the dead and over their human form.
- **Kappa:** the water in their head-dish never dries out, they become stronger on land, and their healing salves (a kappa's secret medicine) become miraculous.
- **Oni:** strength under control. A clumsy oni stops breaking things, and their horns grow.
- **Tengu:** better wings, sharper wind-magic and martial skill. A long-nosed yamabushi tengu's nose grows (to their great pride).
- **Jorōgumo / tsuchigumo:** finer silk that can weave cloth worth a fortune, and a stronger charm.
- **Nure-onna and serpent folk:** a longer, stronger tail, softer scales, and a richer venom or charm.
- **Yuki-onna:** they can finally tolerate warmth, sit by a fire, eat hot food and survive a hug without melting, while keeping their cold power.
- **Ningyo:** longer time on land. Their singing voice could stop a war.
- **Raijū:** controlled lightning; they stop zapping people when excited.
- **Inugami / ōkami:** stronger instincts, and a guardian's sense for danger to their loved ones.
- **Tsukumogami:** their object-body becomes nearly indestructible (a karakasa's paper stops tearing), they can hold human form longer, and their original object's use improves (a blessed teapot's tea heals).
- **Satori:** better control. They can finally turn the mind-reading *off*.
- **Rokurokubi:** their neck stops stretching on its own while they sleep.
- **Noppera-bō:** they can wear a face whenever they choose.
- **Beastfolk:** sharper animal senses and gifts (a wolfkin's nose, a deerfolk's leap, a rabbitfolk's speed), strong health, and a richer coat or plumage.

## Blessing: Humans and Hidden Blood
- **Plain humans** gain a gift that fits who they are: a skill, passion or nature that becomes slightly miraculous.
  - A baker's bread always rises and tastes like home.
  - A runner's lungs never burn.
  - A gambler's tells vanish.
  - A shy artist's hand never shakes.
  - A salaryman's spreadsheets never crash.
  - A grandmother's garden feeds the whole street.
  - A samurai's sword arm never tires.
- **Humans with hidden fantastic blood** (common in Omote, and also found in Ura) *awaken*. Over the next few days the dormant heritage comes to the surface:
  - fox ears in the morning mirror;
  - a tail that pops out during a meeting;
  - frost on a coffee cup;
  - a sudden craving for cucumbers and an urge to wrestle;
  - small horns coming in;
  - an ability to vanish in a crowd.
  The awakening can be partial (a feature or two and a minor power) or full (they become a true half-yokai or full yokai), depending on how strong the blood is. People who didn't know are shocked, and it is played for comedy and drama. People who did know find their heritage fulfilled. Every awakening in Omote gives the Kakure Neighborhood Association another headache (see 07).
- **Humans who are already spiritual** (shrine maidens, monks, onmyōji) find their spiritual power strengthened.

## Blessing: Foreigners and Kami
- **Foreign otherworlders** from Ura-Dejima ripen the same way.
  - A lamia's coils strengthen and she sheds cleanly.
  - A dragon's hoard instinct turns into an uncanny talent for business.
  - An elf's archery becomes perfect.
  - A harpy can fly in a storm.
  - A slime holds her shape.
  - A succubus is finally satisfied (and is shocked).
- **A minor kami** (if one is ever a thread or lover) gains power, more worshippers, and a bigger domain: a roadside god of a single crossroads becomes the god of the whole town.

## The Blessing as a Commodity
Word of an Omote-bito with Izumo's knot spreads fast in Ura (see 08 · Knock-On Effects in Ura). Because of that:

- **Seducers:** courtesans, ambitious clan heirs, aging yokai wanting another tail, and fortune-seekers try to seduce {{user}} for the favor or for a blessed child.
- **Counterfeits:** fake "Musubi charms" and "blessed by the Omote-bito" sake are sold in every market.
- **Protective partners:** thread-partners get protective and, comically, act as {{user}}'s bouncers.
- **Clan politics:** the Great Clans scheme for blessed heirs (see 11).
- **Telling them apart:** {{user}} learns to tell a thread (unmistakable on contact) from a schemer. Inaba is very useful here: she can always see whether someone is a thread.
